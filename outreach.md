I believe **science should be accessible to everyone**, and want to help bridge the gap between jargony explanations and language that anyone at any level can understand. I want to empower both the younger generation to feel like science is exciting and fun and something they can do themselves, as well as the older generation who didn’t go into science but may still have an interest (just ask my dad).

If you're interested in collaborating or hosting me as a speaker, **[please get in touch](#get-in-touch){: .scrolly}**!

## <span class="badge fa-bullhorn"></span>Public talks
<span class="image left">
    <img src="{{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/AstroTours.jpg" alt="Alicia giving an AstroTours public talk on black hole formation" />
</span>
One of my favourite forms of outreach is giving **public talks about black holes, astronomy, and science**.  I have spoken at venues like [Astronomy on Tap Toronto](https://astronomyontap.org/locations/astronomy-on-tap-t-o/), [Sip & Scholar](https://sipandscholar.com/), [AstroTours](https://www.astro.utoronto.ca/astrotours/), and individual classroom visits, reaching audiences ranging from young students to **crowds of hundreds in a pub**.

If you're planning an event and looking for an engaging speaker, please don't hesitate to [contact me](#get-in-touch){: .scrolly} to see if I'd be a good fit!

## <span class="badge fa-users"></span>Discover the Universe
<span class="image right">
    <img src="{{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/DiscoverTheUniverse.jpg" alt="Discover the Universe logo" />
</span>
Discover the Universe is a program run by Canadian astronomers aimed towards making free, bilingual [teacher training](https://www.discovertheuniverse.ca/training) and [classroom resources](https://www.discovertheuniverse.ca/guides) for educators across the country.  I have been working in my role as **Education Specialist** since 2023, when I was hired to help out with preparations for the upcoming **April 8th 2024 total solar eclipse**.  I created eclipse activities and delivered an **Eclipse 101 & 201 workshop series** both in person and online over several months leading up to the big day, through which I reached **thousands of teachers and educators** across Canada and beyond, who were then gifted eclipse glasses for their classrooms.

Since the eclipse, I mainly act in the capacity of a speaker, hosting **regular webinars** on astronomy content for teachers and presenting at **teacher conferences** across the country, including the [Science Teachers' Association of Ontario](https://stao.ca/) conference and the [Nova Scotia Association of Science Teachers](https://sites.google.com/view/ast-nstu) conference in Halifax.  I have continued to help create classroom resources, like [Cosmic Foundations](https://www.discovertheuniverse.ca/foundations), a series of activities teaching the fundamentals of physics required for astronomy. I also plan and host our yearly contest *In an astronomer's shoes*, for which the prize is a classroom visit and one-on-one conversation with a real astronomer (me!).

Our resources extend beyond the classroom and have ties to subjects outside astronomy, like history or maths.  If you're interested, please check us out!

## <span class="badge fa-flag"></span>Leadership
<span class="image left">
    <img src="{{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/AstroToursTeam.jpg" alt="Group photo of the AstroTours outreach team" />
</span>
Outreach is a team effort, and I have had the fortune of working on or leading several initiatives, gaining experience in leading a team and organizing complex events.  I led the **2023 AstroTours Keynote** event featuring famous science communicator [Dr. Katie Mack](https://www.astrokatie.com/) and several activities including an array of astronomy demos, telescope viewing, lab tours, planetarium talks, and a book signing with Dr. Mack, marking our **biggest event to-date** with over 400 guests; I then **co-led the entire AstroTours series** the following year.  I also served as **Curriculum Lead for [Coding the Cosmos](https://github.com/Coding-the-Cosmos)**, a grad student-led astronomy and coding summer camp for underprivileged high school students.  Through programs like [Adventures in Science](https://www.uoftmedsoc.com/clubscommunity/adventures-in-science-%28ais%29-stg) and [Scientifically Yours](https://brocku.ca/mathematics-science/scientificallyyours/), I have also directly led teams of high school students working on gaining a deeper understanding of a scientific concept. I'm also a **regular [science fair](https://www.basef.ca/) judge**, which is one of my favourite events of the year; speaking one-on-one with curious and passionate students always fuels my own excitement for science.
