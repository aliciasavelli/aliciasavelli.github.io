## <span class="badge fa-pencil"></span>Teaching
<span class="image left">
    <img src="{{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/WembleyClassroom.jpg" alt="Alicia's maths classroom at Wembley High Technology College" />
</span>
I am a former **maths and physics teacher**, completing my **B.Ed. practicum in Ontario** and teaching for a year in London at [**Wembley High Technology College**](https://www.whtc.co.uk/), one of the top three schools in the UK for GCSE results.  Although I found teaching incredibly rewarding, I missed doing science myself, so I returned home to Canada to continue my own education through my PhD.

I continue to teach at the post-secondary level as a **teaching assistant for six different astronomy courses**, ranging from introductory astronomy for non-majors ([AST101](https://artsci.calendar.utoronto.ca/course/ast101h1) & [AST201](https://artsci.calendar.utoronto.ca/course/ast201h1)) to computational methods ([CTA200](https://artsci.calendar.utoronto.ca/course/cta200h1)) to upper-year astrophysics courses ([AST320](https://artsci.calendar.utoronto.ca/course/ast320h1)) -- for the latter two I served as **sole TA** for CTA200 and **head TA** for AST320, responsible for organizing assessments and tutorials and, in AST320, delegating tasks to other TAs.  I am most proud of [**AST424**](https://www.astro.utoronto.ca/ast424h1ast425y1/), a first course introducing advanced students to research, whose structure and curriculum I designed together with the instructor.  I have also given **guest lectures**, including *Black Holes 101* for the Summer Undergraduate Research Program at the University of Toronto.

## <span class="badge fa-lightbulb-o"></span>Student supervision
<span class="image right">
    <img src="{{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/Whiteboard.jpg" alt="Whiteboard covered in a colour-coded derivation" />
</span>
I have had the fortune of **co-supervising six undergraduate students** through honours theses and summer research projects.

My most recent student, **Maëlle Magnan**, is extending my fluid cooling model in an upcoming publication, and is now a **PhD student at New York University**.  The remaining five students I led together as a **small research group** from 2023--2026, investigating the evolution of galaxies in star formation histories, and their combined work has resulted in a publication.

If you are interested in working with me, **[please reach out](#get-in-touch){: .scrolly}**!

## <span class="badge fa-compass"></span>Mentorship & service
<span class="image left">
    <img src="{{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/SURPPosterSession.jpg" alt="Students and faculty at the SURP poster session" />
</span>
Beyond my own students, I have **formally mentored two graduate and three undergraduate students** through departmental programs.

Additionally, for the past four years, I have been heavily involved in my department’s **Summer Undergraduate Research Program (SURP)** and the adjoining **Summer Undergraduate Research Fellowship (SURF)** at CITA -- the same program that gave me my first step into astrophysics research when I myself was an undergraduate student -- first as a project supervisor, and now as a **two-time committee member**, planning and presenting lecture series, professional development seminars, Q&A panels, social events, and research showcases & poster fairs. Through this experience, I have directly influenced **multiple cohorts of new researchers**.
